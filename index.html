    What Is a Grade Calculator?

    A grade calculator — also called a grading calculator — is a tool that works out an academic score from assessment results. Depending on the grading method, it can calculate a percentage, an average, or a weighted course grade.

    A single test score is usually calculated from points earned and points possible. A course grade may require combining several assessments, and a weighted grade calculation gives each assessment the influence specified by the syllabus.

    The term "grade calculator" can therefore describe several different calculations. Choose the tool that matches your question:

    How to Calculate Your Grade

    The correct formula depends on how your teacher or institution scores the work. These are the most common methods.

    Method 1

    Calculate a Grade From Points

    When an assessment is marked by points, divide the points earned by the total points possible and multiply by 100.

    Percentage = (Points Earned ÷ Points Possible) × 100

    Worked example — you earn 42 points out of 50

    42 ÷ 50→0.84
    0.84 × 100→84%
    Your score is 84% (B on the standard US scale)

    This points-based method works as a total grade calculator for quizzes, tests, homework, and assignments that have a defined maximum score: add up everything earned, divide by everything possible.

    Method 2

    Calculate an Unweighted Grade Average

    If every assignment has equal importance, add the scores and divide by the number of scores.

    Average = Sum of scores ÷ Number of scores

    Worked example — your scores are 85%, 90%, 78%, and 92%

    85 + 90 + 78 + 92→345
    345 ÷ 4→86.25%
    Your unweighted average is 86.25%

    This method is not appropriate when a final exam or another assessment has a different weight from the rest of your work.

    Method 3

    Calculate a Weighted Grade

    A weighted grade is used when assignments or categories contribute different percentages to the course result — that is when you need a grade calculator with weights.

    Weighted Grade = Σ (Score × Weight) ÷ 100

    Worked example — Homework 92% × 20, Quizzes 85% × 20, Midterm 78% × 25, Final 88% × 35

    18.4 + 17.0 + 19.5 + 30.8→85.7
    The weighted course grade is 85.7%

    If you are calculating a current grade before all assessments are complete, divide the weighted contributions from completed work by the total weight of that completed work. Do not automatically treat ungraded future work as zero unless your course gradebook or instructor does so.

    For a separate calculation that works backward from a target exam result, use the Final Grade Calculator.

    Your current grade is based on the assessments included in the calculation at the time you check it. Your final course grade is the result after all required assessments and course rules have been applied — and the grade that appears on your transcript.

    A learning management system may show a running grade that excludes ungraded assignments, includes them as zeros, or calculates categories in a particular way. Check the gradebook settings and syllabus before comparing its displayed result with a manual calculation.

    Tips for Accurate Grade Calculations

    • Read the syllabus to confirm each assessment's weight.
    • Use the actual score and maximum points for each assessment.
    • Check whether the gradebook excludes ungraded work or treats it as zero.
    • Do not use a simple average when assessments have different weights.
    • Keep full precision during the calculation and round only the final result as required by your institution.
    • Confirm the official letter-grade boundaries and pass requirements with your school, including whether any course is graded pass/fail.
    • Check for special rules such as dropped scores, extra credit, retakes, or a minimum final-exam score.
