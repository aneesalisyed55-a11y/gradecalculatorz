About

This site is a free set of online calculators that work out grades, final exam scores, semester averages and grade point averages. No sign-up, no downloads — just the maths, explained.

What this site does

This site brings together four free tools:

  • Main calculator — work out a current grade from points, percentages, weighted categories or letter grades.
  • Final Grade — find the score needed on a final exam to reach a target course grade, or see the course grade after the exam.
  • Semester Grade — combine quarters, grading periods and semester exams into one semester result.
  • GPA Calculator — calculate a grade point average from course grades and credit hours, or estimate a cumulative GPA across terms.

Each tool explains the formula it uses and shows worked examples, so you can check the result by hand if you want to.

Who it is for

The calculators are made for students checking where they stand, teachers working out grades quickly, and parents helping with schoolwork. They are useful at any level, from high school quarters to university courses — as long as you enter the weights and grade values your own school uses.

How it works

Every calculation runs in your own browser. You type in your scores, the calculator works out the result instantly, and nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored anywhere except on your own device: the main calculator on the homepage remembers your entries in your browser's local storage so they are still there when you come back. You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

There are no accounts, no subscriptions and no paywalls. The reference links on the calculator pages lead to official university pages that describe grading policies and grade calculation.

A note on accuracy

Every result on this site is an estimate based on the numbers you enter. Grading scales, grade-point values and exam weights vary between schools and universities, so the official grade is always the one your school or university records. Check your syllabus, report card or registrar's grading policy before relying on a result.

The site is not affiliated with any school, college or university. University names appear only as sources for the official grading references linked from the calculator pages.
